A Bitcoin address is a unique string of 26 to 35 alphanumeric characters (or starting with `bc1` for newer formats) that
functions like a digital destination or account number for sending and receiving Bitcoin.

- **Analogy:** Think of it like an email address, but for money. If someone wants to send you Bitcoin, they send it to your
  specific address, just like an email goes to your inbox.
- **Common Formats:**
- _Legacy addresses:_ Start with the number `1` (e.g., `1A1zP1eP5QGefi2DMPTfTL5SLmv7DivfNa`).
- _Script addresses:_ Start with the number `3`.
- _Native SegWit (Bech32) addresses:_ Start with `bc1`, which are more efficient and cheaper to use.

- **Public vs. Private:** A Bitcoin address is completely public and safe to share with others. However, it is mathematically
  linked to a **private key** (like a master password), which you must keep entirely secret because whoever controls the
  private key controls the funds at that address.
