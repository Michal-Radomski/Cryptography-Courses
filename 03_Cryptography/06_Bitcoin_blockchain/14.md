Cryptocurrency was created to provide a digital form of money that allows people to send and receive funds directly to one
another anywhere in the world without needing a trusted middleman, such as a bank or government.

The primary goals behind its creation included:

- **Decentralization:** Traditional money is controlled by central banks and governments, which can print more money or
  freeze accounts. Cryptocurrency operates on decentralized computer networks, meaning no single entity controls it.
- **Financial Inclusion:** It aimed to give financial services to the millions of people around the world who lack access to
  traditional banking systems.
- **Censorship Resistance:** Transactions can be made peer-to-peer without the risk of a third party blocking or interfering
  with the transfer.
- **Security and Privacy:** Cryptography is used to secure transactions and protect user data, making digital assets much
  harder to forge or steal compared to traditional digital payments.

Bitcoin, the first cryptocurrency, was launched in 2009 in the wake of the global financial crisis as an alternative monetary
system built on transparency and math rather than trust in financial institutions.
